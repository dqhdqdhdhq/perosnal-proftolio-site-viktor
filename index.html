FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you design and develop?

Yes — that’s the whole point. One person from first sketch to deployed site means nothing gets lost in translation, and the design is never something the code “tries” to match. It just is.

How fast will I see results?

First designs usually land within a week of kickoff. A typical marketing site ships in 3–5 weeks; bigger products in phases. On retainer, requests average a 48-hour turnaround.

What’s your stack?

TypeScript, React/Next.js and Node.js where they earn their keep — and zero-dependency, hand-written HTML/CSS/JS where they don’t. This site is the latter: view source, it’s all there.

How many requests can I make on retainer?

Queue up as many as you like; I work through them one at a time with full focus. Most clients ship 6–10 meaningful pieces a month this way.

Can I pause the retainer?

Anytime. Billing cycles pause with you and unused days roll over. No lock-in, no awkward email required — one click in your dashboard.

Where are you based — and the name “Viktor”?

Earth, CET timezone, fully remote. And yes — just Viktor. Like Madonna, but with more semicolons.
