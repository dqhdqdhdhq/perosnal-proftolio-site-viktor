02 About me

The human behind
the helmet

psst — he’s watching your cursor

Hi, I’m Viktor. I’ve spent the last few years doing one thing: making the web feel less like paperwork and more like play.

I design and build websites, apps, and interfaces end-to-end — from the first sketch to the last git push. My happy place is where engineering meets design: shaving milliseconds, polishing micro-interactions, and sweating details most people only feel rather than see.

When I’m not shipping, I’m probably reverse-engineering an animation I saw somewhere, or explaining to my family that no, I can’t fix the printer. (I can. I just won’t.)
